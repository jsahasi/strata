A WithheldClaim has no statement field, so this template could not render one if it tried; the withheld loop reaches for the reason, the citation address, and the bytes the source really carries at those offsets. That is ADR-003 held up by the shape of the data rather than by the care of whoever edits this file next. THE READING ORDER THIS PAGE IS BUILT FOR, and the reason for every wrapper in it: what changed, then the claim that rests on it, then the citation under that claim, then -- last, and with the interface going flat and still -- the claim the system refused to make. Three movements, one column each, no instruction anywhere telling the reader that is the order. Three rules for anyone changing it. 1. Never add {{ claim.statement }} to the withheld loop. Render them hidden instead and a reader with scripts blocked gets the claim and none of its evidence. 3. Do not lift the withheld claims out into a section of their own.
