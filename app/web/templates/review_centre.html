Sources

Where the evidence came from. External is the public record the company will be held to; internal is the company's own account of itself, which can be out of date and carries no authority with a regulator. A source marked as vouched for is a person's judgement about the document. It is not a verification result and never stands in for one.

{# ------------------------------------------------------- collective take -- #}

Collective take

The synthesis, and directly above it the coverage it was composed against. A take that says "rests on nine findings" and a take that says "rests on nine, excludes four that failed verification" are different documents, and only the second one can be handed to a regulator. The counts are the ones written when the take was composed, not recomputed now.
