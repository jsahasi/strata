Strata

Regulatory change, carried through to a citation you can check.

A platform for regulatory affairs teams at multi-state utilities. It reads successive versions of a proceeding, establishes exactly what changed, maps each change to the obligations and filings it touches, and routes an action to a named reviewer.

The part worth explaining is what it refuses to do. Every statement is bound to an exact character span in the source filing and re-checked against that text before it reaches a screen. A statement whose citation fails that check is withheld — not greyed out, not shown with a confidence score and a disclaimer. Withheld.
