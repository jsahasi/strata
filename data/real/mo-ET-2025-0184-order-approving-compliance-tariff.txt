          ORDER APPROVING TARIFF REVISION

Issue Date: December 19, 2025                          Effective Date: January 4, 2026

    On December 5, 2025, Union Electric Company d/b/a Ameren Missouri filed

proposed tariff revision, Tracking No. JE-2026-0076, which bears an effective date of

January 4, 2026, to comply with the Commission’s November 24, 2025, order authorizing

a Large Load Rate Plan for Ameren Missouri. Staff filed a recommendation to approve

the tariff revision. No other responses were received.

     The Commission has reviewed the tariff revision and Staff's recommendation. The

Commission finds that tariff revision should be approved. The Commission will approve

Ameren  Missouri’s  tariff  revision  to become  effective on January  4, 2026. The

Commission finds it reasonable to make this order effective in less than 30 days so that

the order will be effective concurrently with the compliance tariff revision.

    THE COMMISSION ORDERS THAT:

       1.    Ameren Missouri’s tariff revision, assigned Tracking No. JE-2026-0076, is

approved to become effective on January 4, 2026.

       2.     This order shall become effective on January 4, 2026.                          BY THE COMMISSION
