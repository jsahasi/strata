DUKE ENERGY CAROLINAS, 
LLC’S AND DUKE ENERGY 
PROGRESS, LLC’S MOTION 
REQUESTING A LIMITED 
WAIVER OF COMMISSION 
RULE R1-17B(C) 

NOW COMES Duke Energy Carolinas, LLC (“DEC”) and Duke Energy Progress, 

LLC  (“DEP”  or  together  as  the  “Companies”),  pursuant  to  North  Carolina  Utilities 

Commission  (“Commission”)  Rules  R1-5  and  R1-7,  and  respectfully  request  a  limited 

waiver of Commission Rule 17B(c) during the pendency of the Commission’s decision on 

the proposed revisions to Commission Rule R1-17B in Docket No. E-100, Sub 178.  

In support of this Motion, DEC and DEP respectfully show the Commission the 

following:  

1. 

On  September  20,  2024,  the  Public  Staff  –  North  Carolina  Utilities 

Commission  (the  “Public  Staff”)  filed  a  Petition  to  Amend  Commission  Rule  R1-17B 

(“Petition”). Among other things, the Public Staff’s Petition included a proposal to adjust 

1 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
the timing of the technical conference on transmission and distribution projects. The Public 

Staff noted that under current Rule R1-17B, an electric public utility must file a request to 

initiate a technical conference no later than 120 days before the filing of a performance-

based regulation (“PBR”) application. The Public Staff proposed instead that an electric 

public utility be required to file the request for initiation of a technical conference at least 

30 days before filing the notice of intent to file a PBR application, in order to have the most 

current  information  and  underlying  data  available  for  the  technical  conference  and  in 

accompanying documents and presentations.  

2. 

Consistent with the Commission’s September 25, 2024 Order Requesting 

Comments on Proposed Revisions to Commission Rule R1-17B, the Companies filed initial 

Comments  on  the  proposed  revisions  to  Rule  R1-17B  on  October  24,  2024,  and  Reply 

Comments on December 6, 2024 (collectively, “Comments”).  

3. 

In its Comments, the Companies agreed with the Public Staff’s proposal to 

revise R1-17B(c), in relevant part, as follows:  

(c)  Technical  Conference.  –  No  later  than  90  At  least  30  days  before  an 

electric public utility gives notice that it intends to file a general rate case 

that includes a PBR application, the electric public utility shall file a request 

with  the  Commission  to  initiate  a  technical  conference  regarding  the 

projected transmission and distribution projects to be included in the PBR 

application.  The  Commission  will  schedule  one  or  more  sessions  of  the 

technical conference to be conducted within 60 days of receiving a request 

for a technical conference. no later than the projected date of filing of the 

PBR application. 

2 

 
 
4. 

5. 

No party opposed the Public Staff’s proposed revision to Rule R1-17B(c). 

Under the existing Rule R1-17B(c), the Companies would be required to 

file their requests to initiate technical conferences at least 90 days before giving notices of 

their intent to file general rate cases that include PBR applications, as authorized under 

N.C. Gen. Stat. §62-133.16. Therefore, the Companies request limited waivers of Rule R1-

17B(c) that require the Companies to file requests to initiate technical conferences no later 

than 90 days before they give notices that they intend to file general rate cases and PBR 

applications (the “Motion for Limited Waiver”).  

6. 

Instead, during the pendency of the Commission’s decision on the proposed 

revisions to Rule R1-17B, the Companies request that they be directed to file their requests 

to initiate a technical conference at least 30 days before they give notices of their intent to 

file general rate cases and PBR applications, consistent with the Public Staff’s proposed 

revisions and the Companies’ comments.   

7. 

In the alternative, should the Commission decline to grant limited waivers 

of the technical conference filing requirements currently included in Rule R1-17B(c), the 

Companies  respectfully  request  that  the  Commission  treat  this  motion  as  a  request  to 

initiate technical conferences regarding the projected transmission and distribution projects 

to be included in the Companies’ next PBR applications.   

8. 

The Companies discussed this matter with the Public Staff and they do not 

object to the Companies’ Motion for Limited Waiver. 

WHEREFORE,  Duke  Energy  Carolinas,  LLC  and  Duke  Energy  Progress,  LLC 

respectfully request that the Commission issue an Order: (i) granting a limited waiver of 

Commission  Rule  R1-17B(c)’s  requirement  that  the  Companies  file  requests  to  initiate 

3 

 
 
technical conferences no later than 90 days before giving their notices that they intend to 

file  general  rate  case  and  PBR  applications  and  (ii)  direct  the  Companies  to  file  their 

requests to initiate technical conferences at least 30 days before they give notices of their 

intent to file general rate cases that includes PBR applications.
