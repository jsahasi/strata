16 Q. FOR BACKGROUND, WHAT ARE CUSTOMER CLASSIFICATIONS AND HOW
17 ARE THEY USED IN THE RATE SETTING PROCESS?
Customer classifications, also referred to as “customer classes,” represent broad general18 A.
groups of utility customers that are typically separated and organized based upon the19
service characteristics of the customer. Customer classes form the basis of a utility's class20
COS study and represent the starting point for developing customer rates. Specifically, a21
utility's costs and revenues are first assigned to each customer class within the utility's class22
COS study. A utility's revenue requirement is then apportioned to each customer class,23
based upon principles of cost causation, from which customer billing rates are developed24
to recover this class apportioned revenue.25
