 2       (A)   Revenue Requirement and ROE

 3         Q.  WHAT IS YOUR UNDERSTANDING OF THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED

 4           REVENUE REQUIREMENT INCREASE?

 5         A.   My understanding is that the Company is seeking an annual revenue increase of

 6                 approximately $226.1 million, or 11.5 percent.3 This proposed increase is based on

 7                 a 12-month forecasted test period ending December 31, 2026,4 as supported by a

 8               12-month base period ending August 31, 2025, and consisting of six months of

 9                   actual data (September 1, 2024 through February 28, 2025) and six months of

10                  forecasted data (March 1, 2025 through August 31, 2025).5

11         Q.  WHAT IS THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED ROE IN THIS CASE?

12         A.    The Company proposes an ROE of 10.95 percent based on a range of 10.32 percent

13                   to 11.84 percent.6 In addition, the Company proposes a capital structure of 46.15

14                  percent debt and 52.86 percent equity for ratemaking purposes.7 Applying the

15               Company's proposed ROE to its proposed capital structure, the Company is asking

16                  the Commission to approve a Rate of Return equal to 8.10 percent based on 13-

17               month weighted average cost of capital ("WACC").8

12                 assigned to classes, social goals associated with the role of the prices in a particular

13                    jurisdiction, and response to the state of the economy within or external to the

14                  regulatory jurisdiction. The Commission may exercise its discretion based on one

15                  or more of these concerns to adjust revenue allocation to support policy or advance

16                  the public interest. However, these adjustments often result in rates that are not

17                  cost-based and, as a result, may not be just, reasonable, and equitable.
