               ORDER NUNC PRO TUNC

Issue Date: December 3, 2025                       Effective Date: December 3, 2025

     The Commission is correcting its Order Regarding Ameren Missouri’s Request for

Approval   of  a  Large  Load  Rate  Plan  and  Associated  Variance,  issued

November 24, 2025, nunc pro tunc pursuant to Commission Rule 20 CSR 4240-2.160(4)

to correct an error.

    On page four of the order under Revenue Sharing the Commission summarized

the revenue sharing provision as follows:

     Revenue Sharing – The Agreement provides that Ameren Missouri shall
         file a yearly Earnings Review Surveillance report. If that report shows that
      the revenues from large load customers exceed the amount of large load
      customers base rate revenues, such excess revenue shall be returned to
      customers in an amortization to be determined in a future rate case. Such
      revenue shall be used to benefit all customers of the electric utility, and
      excess revenue amounts above a fixed percentage shall be set aside
       exclusively for the benefit of low-income customers.

    On December 1, 2025, Union Electric Company d/b/a Ameren Missouri filed

Motion for Order Nunc Pro Tunc or, Alternatively, for Clarification or Rehearing. Ameren

Missouri states that the Commission’s summary leaves out a necessary step explaining

how such excess revenue is determined. The Commission agrees.

     The Commission’s summary  of the revenue sharing provision  of  its order

approving the Amended Non-Unanimous Global Stipulation and Agreement (Agreement)was meant to accurately reflect how the Revenue Sharing provision would work. The

Commissions summary failed to adequately describe how revenue sharing would be

calculated. Revenue sharing would be calculated as explained in paragraph 46 of the

Agreement, and an example of such calculation is included in Exhibit D attached to the

Agreement.

     The Commission will correct the orders Revenue Sharing paragraph to reference

paragraph 46 and Exhibit D of the stipulation and agreement, which explain the revenue

sharing mechanism. Therefore, the Revenue Sharing paragraph on page four  is

corrected to read as follows:

     Revenue Sharing – The Agreement provides that Ameren Missouri shall
         file a yearly Earnings Review Surveillance report. Paragraph 46 of the
      Agreement and Exhibit D, attached to the Agreement, explain and provide
     an example of the revenue sharing mechanism and the process to quantify
      any revenues to be returned  to customers  in an amortization to be
      determined in a future rate case. Such revenue shall be used to benefit all
      customers of the electric utility, and excess revenue amounts above a fixed
      percentage shall be set aside exclusively for the benefit of low-income
      customers.

      This Order Nunc Pro Tunc corrects that Revenue Sharing paragraph in its prior

Order Regarding Ameren Missouri’s Request for Approval of a Large Load Rate Plan

and Associated Variance.

    THE COMMISSION ORDERS THAT:

       1. The Order Regarding Ameren Missouri’s Request for Approval of a Large Load

Rate Plan and Associated Variance, issued November 24, 2025, is corrected nunc pro

tunc as set forth in the body of this order.
