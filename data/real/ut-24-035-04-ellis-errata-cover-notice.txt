       This Errata is to correct the witness and exhibit numbers in the Office of Consumer Services’

(OCS) witness Mark Ellis’s Phase III Direct Testimony, his Exhibit OCS 6.1D (Qualifications), and his

Exhibit OCS 6.2D (White Paper). In addition, these errata correct two lines of Mr. Ellis’s testimony and

the title to figure 4 contained in the White Paper. Specifically, the top of the title page of testimony reads:

“Ellis Phase III OCS – 2D.”  It should read: “Ellis Phase III OCS – 6D.” The header of every page in the

testimony reads: “Ellis Phase III OCS – 2D.”  It should read: “Ellis Phase III OCS – 6D.” Page 1 line 13

of Mr. Ellis’s testimony reads: “Yes, I have prepared Exhibits OCS 2.1D through 2.2D . . . .”  It should

read: “Yes, I have prepared Exhibits OCS 6.1D through 6.2D. . . .” Page 3 line 42, reads: “I am includingin this testimony, as Exhibit 2.2D, . . . .”  It should read: “I am including in this testimony, as Exhibit

6.1D, . . .” Page 6 line 122 reads: “I am including in this testimony, as Exhibit 2.1D, . . .”  It should read:

“I am including in this testimony, as Exhibit 6.2D, . . .” The top of the title page of the While Paper

reads: “Phase III Exhibit OCS 2.1D.”  It should read: “Phase III Exhibit OCS 6.2D.” The top right corner

of the first page of the Qualifications reads “Phase III Exhibit OCS 2.2D.”  It should read: “Phase III

Exhibit OCS 6.1D.”

        In addition to the corrections of witness and exhibit numbering, page 24 line 488 of Mr. Ellis’s

testimony reads: “allow PacifiCorp to recover insurable losses are recoverable in rates.”  It should read:

“allow PacifiCorp to recover insurable losses in rates.” Page 6 line 122-23 reads: “whitepaper

commissioned by the Utah Office of Consumer Services.”  It should read: “white paper commissioned by

the Utah Office of Consumer Services.” Finally, page 19 of Mr. Ellis’s White Paper contains a title to

figure 4 that reads: “State electricity sales and illustrative wildfire liability risk.”  It should read:

“PacifiCorp state share shares of electricity sales, revenue, and illustrative wildfire liability risk.”

      The OCS provides a red line and a clean complete copy of Mr. Ellis’s Phase III Direct Testimony

and two exhibits. We apologize for any confusion these mistakes may have caused.
