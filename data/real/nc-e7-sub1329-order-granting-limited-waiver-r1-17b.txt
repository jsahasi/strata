ORDER GRANTING MOTION 
FOR LIMITED WAIVER OF 
COMMISSION RULE R1-17B(c) 

BY THE CHAIR: On July 8, 2025, Duke Energy Carolinas, LLC (DEC), and Duke 
Energy  Progress,  LLC  (DEP,  together  with  DEC,  Duke),  filed  a  motion  pursuant  to 
Commission Rules R1-5 and R1-7 requesting a limited waiver of a provision of Commission 
Rule  R1-17B(c)  (Motion  for  Limited  Waiver)  during  the  pendency  of  the  Commission’s 
decision regarding proposed revisions to Commission Rule R1-17(B) in Docket No. E-100, 
Sub 178. 

Duke  explained  that  on  September  20,  2024,  the  Public  Staff  filed  a  Petition  to 
Amend Commission Rule R1-17B (PBR Rule) in Docket No. E-100, Sub 178 (Petition). 
In its Petition, the Public Staff proposed among other things, that the Commission adjust 
the  timing  of  the  technical  conference  on  transmission  and  distribution  projects  in 
Performance-Based  Regulation  (PBR)  rate  cases.  Duke  stated  that  Rule  R1-17B(c) 
currently  requires  that  an  electric  public  utility  file  a  request  to  initiate  a  technical 
conference  no  later  than  90  days  before  the  filing  of  a  PBR  application.  The  rule  also 
currently requires that the Commission schedule “one or more sessions of the technical 
conference”  to  be  conducted  within  60  days  of  receiving  a  request  for  a  technical 
conference. The Public Staff proposed in its Petition that the Commission amend the rule 
to require an electric public utility to file the request for initiation of a technical conference 
at least 30 days before filing the notice of intent to file a PBR application so that the most 

current information and underlying data will be available for the technical conference and 
the accompanying documents and presentations.  

Duke stated that it filed initial comments and reply comments regarding the Public 
Staff’s proposed revisions to the PBR Rule on October 24, 2024, and December 6, 2024, 
respectively. Duke further stated that it agreed with the Public Staff’s proposal regarding 
the  timing  of  the  filing  of  a  request  for initiation  of  a  technical  conference. Specifically, 
Duke agreed that Rule R1-17B(c) should be amended to require the filing of the request 
to  initiate  a  technical  conference  at  least  30  days  (rather  than  90  days  as  currently 
required by the rule) before an electric public utility gives notice that it intends to file a 
general  rate  case  that  includes  a  PBR  application.  Duke  also  agreed  with  the  Public 
Staff’s proposal to amend the rule to require that the Commission schedule one or more 
sessions of the technical conference no later than the projected date of filing of the PBR 
application.  Duke  noted  that  no  party  in  Docket  No.  E-100,  Sub  178,  opposed  these 
particular Public Staff proposals for revision of Rule R1-17B(c). 

In the Motion for Limited Waiver, Duke requested a limited waiver of the provision 
of  Rule  R1-17B(c)—that  requires  an  electric  public  utility  to  file  a  request  to  initiate  a 
technical conference no later than 90 days before the filing of the PBR application—so 
that DEC and DEP could file their requests to initiate a technical conference no later than 
30 days before they file notices of their intent to file general rate cases that include PBR 
applications.  However,  if  the  Commission  were  to  deny  the  Motion  for  Limited  Waiver, 
Duke proposed that the Commission treat the Motion for Limited Waiver as a request to 
initiate  technical  conferences  regarding  the  projected  transmission  and  distribution 
projects to be included in Duke’s next PBR applications. 

Duke stated that it has discussed its request with the Public Staff, and the Public 

Staff does not oppose the Motion for Limited Waiver.  

Considering the foregoing, the Chair finds good cause to grant Duke’s Motion for 
Limited Waiver of the Commission Rule R1-17B(c) requirement that electric public utilities 
file requests to initiate technical conferences no later than 90 days before giving notice 
that they intend to file a general rate case with a PBR application. The Chair directs DEP 

2 

and DEC to file their requests to initiate technical conferences at least 30 days before 
they file notices of intent to file general rate cases that include PBR applications. 

IT IS, THEREFORE, SO ORDERED.
