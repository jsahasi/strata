G.3 – Interconnections
The Operating Companies are interconnected with 14 non-associated utilities through 68 different transmission facilities. These transmission lines are operated at voltages of 46 kV, 69 kV, 115 kV, 161 kV, 230 kV, and 500 kV, and include facilities that are operated normally open.
