Build Board

48 hours from 2026-08-03. Ordered so that the thing reviewers execute exists early and improves, rather than arriving at the end.

Start these two now — they have external latency and everything else waits on nothing.
1. Book the user interviews (T1). Two or three regulatory-affairs people. The prep guide calls this the single most useful thing you can do, and it is the only task you cannot compress by working harder. Send the requests before you write a line of code.
2. Confirm the DeepInterview window. The assignment expires 72 hours after the challenge email — 24 hours after the build deadline. The interview starts within 30 minutes of submitting, so plan submission and interview as one sitting.

Cut list — if time runs short

Sacrifice in this order, and say so in the submission rather than hiding it: multi-jurisdiction taxonomy, rollback (keep audit history, drop the restore), the reviewer role model (one reviewer role instead of several), UI polish. Never cut: citation verification, the deterministic diff, draft-versus-final, or the user interviews. Those four are the submission's argument.
