Submission Record

Kept current as the build proceeds. The reply template AI Fund asks for, plus the working notes behind each line.

Write these entries as they happen, not at hour 47. The AI interview asks you to name exact files, functions and commands, and AI Fund reconciles your answers against the repository afterwards. A log written from memory at the end is both weaker and less accurate, and the gap shows.

Running log: AI use

The rubric is explicit — using an AI coding tool is expected; letting it choose the architecture is not. The defensible answer has the shape "I chose this approach because X, and asked the AI to implement it." Record the decisions you made yourself, and the places you rejected what the tool proposed. The rejections are the most valuable rows here: they are evidence of judgement rather than acceptance.

On the 48-hour deadline. If the build needs longer, the instructions say to flag it and give an ETA. Flagging early with a specific ETA reads as judgement; going silent and arriving late reads as neither. Decide by hour 30, not hour 47.

Known gaps to state plainly

List what you did not build and why. A submission that names its own limits is stronger than one that hopes they go unnoticed, and the interview will find them regardless. Saying "I don't know" is described in the prep guide as a positive signal.
